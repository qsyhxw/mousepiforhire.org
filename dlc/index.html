The DLC picture is real, but it is not fully playable yet

The most important thing to understand is that the DLC conversation currently has two different layers. One layer is already live and easy to verify: the Comic Book and Original Game Soundtrack are released and sold separately on Steam. The other layer is the future-facing one: the first Story DLC is listed publicly, included in deluxe packaging, and described as a new adventure for Jack Pepper, but it still does not have a checked public release date.

That means this page works best as a clean DLC hub rather than a fake-finished DLC walkthrough. We can responsibly track what is known now, and we can leave the future expansion lanes ready for the moment the Story DLC actually lands.

For players deciding whether the deluxe bundle is worth it, the useful question is not just "Is there DLC?" It is "What part is playable now, what part is later, and what extra formats are actually included?" That is the exact gap this page fills.

DLC Status

What each add-on currently looks like in the checked public record

The cards below separate released extras from future content so the current DLC state stays easy to read.

Unreleased Expansion

Story DLC

Steam lists the first Story DLC at $9.99. The checked wording says it is a brand new adventure for Jack Pepper and that it will be released at a later date.

At the moment, we do not have a checked public release date, DLC trophy list, DLC collectible list, or mission breakdown to publish as settled fact.

Released Add-On

Comic Book

The Comic Book released on April 16, 2026 and is currently priced at $0.99 on Steam.

It is described as an exclusive digital comic inspired by one of the in-game collectible lines, which gives it more of a companion-piece role than an expansion-mechanics role.

Released Add-On

Original Game Soundtrack

The soundtrack also released on April 16, 2026 and is priced at $9.99 on Steam.

The checked store page credits Patryk Scelina and says the release includes 19 original compositions plus Good Mouse, a track tied to Caravan Palace.

Bundle Value

How the deluxe packaging is framed in the checked sources

The store and official pre-purchase messaging line up cleanly here. The Digital Deluxe Edition includes the base game, the future Story DLC, the Comic Book, and the Original Game Soundtrack. The Digital Deluxe Upgrade is the same extras bundle for players who already own the base game.

That makes the buying decision pretty straightforward. If you only care about playable content today, the value story is weaker because the future expansion is still unreleased. If you already know you want the soundtrack, the comic, and access to the first expansion as soon as it arrives, then the deluxe packaging makes more sense.

Because the checked public details are clearest on Steam, this page currently treats Steam as the safest DLC reference point. If other platform storefronts later publish cleaner add-on breakdowns, this hub can expand with platform-specific sections.
