## Mouse PI For Hire Tammy Tumbler Character Guide

Tammy Tumbler is one of the clearest support characters in MOUSE: P.I. For Hire's public cast material, but the safest way to write her is through function first. The strongest verified version is this: Tammy is an intelligent and resourceful ally, voiced by Camryn Grimes, who serves as Jack's right-hand by tinkering with inventions, helping upgrade his weapons, and showing up through practical side-job support.

## What is actually confirmed

### Tammy Tumbler is part of the main cast

Official cast rollout and launch-window media coverage both treat Tammy as one of the named central figures around Jack Pepper.

### Camryn Grimes voices Tammy Tumbler

Current official cast material and supporting press coverage agree on Camryn Grimes as Tammy's voice actor.

### Her clearest official role is practical support

The strongest current public wording does not overcomplicate Tammy. It frames her as Jack's right-hand, especially through inventions and weapon-upgrade help.

### She also appears in the side-job layer

Current guide coverage and official patch notes both support Tammy as a real side-job giver, not just background flavor around the upgrade loop.

## How to read Tammy Tumbler as a character

### She is the story's utility-and-ingenuity character

If Jack Pepper anchors the noir detective lane and Wanda Fuller anchors the corruption-exposure lane, Tammy helps anchor the problem-solving lane. She keeps the world mechanically alive.

### Her function is material, not just emotional

Tammy matters because she changes what Jack can do. Upgrades, inventions, and practical side-job rewards all make her one of the characters whose presence is felt in the player's hands, not only in dialogue.

### She gives Mouseburg a maker-energy counterweight

Mouseburg is full of rot, corruption, and theatrical dirt. Tammy adds a more constructive energy to that ecosystem: repair, tinkering, iteration, and help that actually changes the run.
