## Mouse PI For Hire John Brown Character Guide

John Brown is one of the clearest public examples of how MOUSE: P.I. For Hire wants Mouseburg to feel lived in rather than abstract. The strongest verified version is this: John Brown is voiced by Fred Tatasciore, publicly described as the proprietor of the Little & Big pub in the heart of Mouseburg, and framed as an overall titan of hard work.

## What is actually confirmed

### John Brown is part of the main cast

Official cast rollout and launch-window coverage both treat John Brown as one of the named central figures around Mouseburg.

### Fred Tatasciore voices John Brown

Current official cast material and supporting media coverage agree on Fred Tatasciore as John Brown's voice actor.

### His strongest public identity is Little & Big pub proprietor

The safest current wording is that John Brown is the proprietor of the Little & Big pub in the heart of Mouseburg. That is cleaner and more stable than expanding him into narrower job labels the official material does not lock in.

### His public flavor is work, place, and local weight

The phrase overall titan of hard work gives John Brown a grounded civic feel. He reads less like a floating archetype and more like one of the characters who make Mouseburg feel socially inhabited.

## How to read John Brown as a character

### He is one of the city's fixed social anchors

Jack Pepper gives the city its detective path. Wanda Fuller gives it journalism. Cornelius Stilton gives it politics. John Brown gives it a place where everyday Mouseburg still has weight, routine, and human traffic.

### He works best as a neighborhood force, not a lore dump

The strongest public sources support John Brown as a venue-linked, labor-coded character. They do not yet support a giant spoiler-heavy biography or a fixed tactical role in every mission discussion.

### His importance is spatial as much as personal

Because John Brown is tied to a named pub in the heart of Mouseburg, he helps make the world feel mapped through real hangouts and recurring local spaces rather than only through combat arenas and case objectives.
