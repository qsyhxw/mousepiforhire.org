## Mouse PI For Hire Jack Squat Cutoff Guide

Jack Squat is not just another late mission name. In the current launch-window route literature, it is the practical cutoff that changes how you should think about the whole run. The safest cross-checked summary is simple: if something still needs the **P.I. Office**, the **main hub**, or the broader pre-endgame cleanup rhythm, assume it should be handled before starting Jack Squat.

## What is actually confirmed

### Jack Squat is a late mission

Checked mission-list sources place Jack Squat after **Glugging From the Deep** and before **The House of the Empty Mouse**. That means you are already in the back third of the campaign by the time this question matters.

### It functions as the practical point of no return for the hub

The clearest current wording comes from PowerPyx: once you start Jack Squat, you can no longer access the main hub. GameSpot reaches the same practical conclusion by telling players to finish the Baseball Cards minigame before this mission because it is the point of no return.

### This does not mean the game instantly runs out of content

The careful wording matters here. Jack Squat does **not** mean the campaign becomes empty. Late missions after it still have their own content. What changes is the safety of **hub-sensitive** progress, side-job timing, bar access logic, and anything else you were still hoping to clean up in a more relaxed office rhythm.

## What should be done before Jack Squat

### Side jobs

Current checked sources agree on **14 side jobs**, most of which are accepted at the P.I. Office before entering their tied mission. Since the game also lacks comfortable replay and level-select cleanup, missed side jobs are one of the clearest reasons to treat Jack Squat seriously.

### Baseball Cards minigame and Raygun path

This is the biggest explicitly documented pre-cutoff task. GameSpot says to play the Baseball Cards minigame before starting Jack Squat, and PowerPyx goes even further by recommending a save right after **Glugging From the Deep** so you can still finish **S'all in the Cards** and **Everybody Loves Rayguns** before committing.

### Any hub-dependent loose ends you still care about

If your plan still depends on checking the bar, talking to office NPCs, accepting available side jobs, or using the calmer hub loop to verify progress, the honest answer is that Jack Squat is too late to still be vague about it.

## The best save strategy around the cutoff

### Keep a pre-Jack-Squat anchor save

The safest route habit is to keep one stable manual save before starting Jack Squat at all.

### Keep an extra post-Glugging save if cards are unfinished

PowerPyx specifically supports this habit for card-related cleanup. If you still need Baseball Card wins, prize-token progress, or the X1 D-Mousifier route, that extra buffer save is worth more than improvising later.
