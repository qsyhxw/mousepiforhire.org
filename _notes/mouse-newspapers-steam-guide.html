INTRODUCTION

"Every case leaves a trail. Every achievement tells a story."

'There is NO returning to hub after beating the game, NO new game+, and NO carry over of upgrades OR collectibles, when starting a new game.

This means that in order to get any collectible achievements (or any achievements that count progress) it means that it all MUST be done in a single playthrough. Save as often as you can, since you aren't currently able to replay missions. Everything is one and done! Hope this helps anyone that was curious about that.

HOWEVER the 'Across one playthrough' achievements are vulnerable to save-reload abuse. Sp33d0n, a commenter, got the Boom Town and Firestarter achievements, by reloading the arena I suggested to do the Hole-y Trinity achievement in.

ALSO if your looking to collect all baseball cards, there are several that are only sold in the ammo store, back at the PI office, which means they will become completely unobtainable upon starting the 1934 World Fair Mission. - Information courtesy of OxfordTraveler

This is a comprehensive BUT LIVE, in-depth guide, detailing a way of getting all achievements currently available in the game. If you have any questions, notes, or additions that can be made to the guide then please let me know in the comments. I would really appreciate it.

I am going to try help us all find every comic, newspaper and baseball card in the game, as we progress. I have been made aware in the comments that any you have missed will become available in the shop for purchasing when returning to the P.I Office Central Hub.

I am also going to try note all of the weapon schematics possible, however I will have missed some as I am doing this guide alone and updating it as I go along. FORTUNATELY, we only need 72 to complete the achievement, requiring all weapon upgrades, and I understand there is well over 90 in the game.

Always exhaust all dialogue options at the central hub (P.I Office) before continuing onto any further missions as you may miss some side jobs. I believe currently I have missed one near the start and wouldn't like any of you to make the same error.

As far as the miscellaneous quests are concerned, up to press, they seem fairly easy. Just bear them in mind as you work through the game and you shouldn't miss any. I will also make note of particularly advantages areas, to try achieve them.

Happy hunting detectives!
DER HARZERBURG / BIG MOUSE, LITTLE HOPE
COMIC LOCATION #1:

Right after the roulette wheel, you will go up some stairs and before progressing you will see a vent, turn back and enter the vent. Press x to use your flashlight.

COMIC LOCATION #2:

As soon as you see the perp we are chasing escaping down in the elevator, go up the stairs instead and there is the second comic at the top.

WEAPON SCHEMATIC #1:
Originally posted by Sp33d0n:
Thought you might like to know there is a Weapon Schematic in the very first level, in a safe with a movement limit and a timer. I believe it's right before the area you recommend to get Goud'em.

Just to add to this, on my second run of the game, I ascertained it is once we reach the bottom of the stairs and cannot go down any further. The first room you enter, hidden in the left hand corner of where you came in.

GOUD'EM!

Immediately, after speaking about the noise of the engines and the wind, you can kite the two enemies toward the container, line them up and kill them both with the micer, then kill the following two that come out of the container. Dash over to the container on the other side of the ship and kill the first and you should easily be able to pick up the GOUD'EM achievement. If you failed to do so reload a manual save you made before the cutscene.


You just put the Chicago overcoat on 'em, detective.
VAUDEVILLE / THE VANISHING ACT
MOUSEBERG HERALD #1:

Immediately after speaking to the MPD Lieutenant, you can go through the door and look to the right. In the locker you will find the first newspaper.

COMIC LOCATION #3:

Upon locating / retrieving the backroom key, in the water tank, there will be an MPD Rookie to your right. Go towards him and turn right into one of the aisles and the comic will be on one of the seats.

MISSABLE CLUE #1 / BANDEL'S POSTER

One of the clues is story related, so you cannot miss it, however, the other is 'Bandel's Poster'. This is located on the Western wall of the same room, next to a top hat and suitcase.

BASEBALL CARD #1:

Upon reading a poster in a dining hall informing you about the importance of armour. You will kill two enemies. Proceed up a flight of stairs and it will be situated on the low table on your right. Charlie Pepper (Pitcher).

There is a further piece of armour found the garage, after the speeding van sequence, where the group of mice ambush you.

''Consider this ‘insurance.’ If you don’t buy it, you’ll find out why it’s worth having Detective.''

BASEBALL CARD #2:
Originally posted by ghosttheinvader:
Continuing up the stairs, if you go out the fire escape, through the window, you can find a secret room. It will be on a wooden stool, next to a door which is being blocked by a crate.

This one is Joel Blunt (Pitcher), up to throw!

WEAPON SCHEMATIC #2:

Shoot the barrel, grab the key in the bathroom and then open the loft door. This is located on the landing where you just came from. This gives you access to a weapon schematic and the dough on the fire escape.

Nice one detective.

HOLE-Y TRINITY:

Just after you are prompted to kick a barrel into two enemies, you have two more to access. Hold one of the barrels, kite the enemies towards each other and then throw it at the group. The explosion of barrel will get you this achievement if you caught 3 in the blast.




COMIC LOCATION #4:

In the same location, clear out the enemies but DO NOT go down into the subway just yet. Make your way to the end of the street and on a bench you will find the fourth comic book, alongside a Heal-D bottle, on a bench.

Put the funny papers down and finish up your job!

WEAPON SCHEMATIC #3:

Down in the station, past the public transport, you will come to a typewriter to save and besides it, a weapon schematic again.

WEAPON SCHEMATIC #4:

After you arrive at the open train carriages, clear out the enemies and head alongside the train, NOT UPSTAIRS. You will turn right into the rundown privy and Mike Maple will be stood in front of another weapon schematic.

WEAPON SCHEMATIC #5:
Originally posted by Blergian:
There's another weapon schematic in the Vaudeville section, if you go through the train car, after the fight, there's a safe on the far left side with a one in it.

To add further clarity. This is still in the same area as the previous schematic. As you're progressing through the carriage there will be an open door onto the train tracks, you have to follow them down, to get to the safe.

MOUSEBERG HERALD #2:

Once you reach the apartment and are ready to speak to Betty-Deux Fiddle, move past her and, on top of the kitchen counter, and you will see the daily edition sitting on the side.
P.I OFFICE
SECRET COLLECTIBLE #1:

In the Little & Big, where you can speak to Wanda Fuller & John Brown, the barman, you can enter the kitchen through a door. Once in the kitchen move to the back and there will be a tailpick opportunity, succeed in this and move down to the basement where the secret figurine will be behind some curtains.

POCKET ACES:

In the same area, the Little & Big bar, we will find a lady wanting the play baseball cards. Sit down, follow the instructions and hopefully win your first game. This will get you your achievement and 1/20 tokens for a 'special prize'. At this point I had $556, enough to play 11 times.

You bet that lady a fat man.

We were holding on to Austin "Cur" Bunz (Batter).

SIDE JOB #1 / SHOW SOME CULTURE, JACK:

As you make your way back to your car you will see Tammy Tumbler stood on the corner. Initiate a conversation with her and exhaust the dialogue. It is at this point you will pick up the concerned quest.

After speaking to Roland or 'The Stage Designer', as he is affectionately referred to, you will enter a room and find the boomstick, kill some enemies and at the end of the room there is a table with cards on. Next to the light stand. Joe Jody Gain (Pitcher)

FIRESTARTER, BOOM TOWN & SPIKE-D 16 TONS:

After we progress down the hallway and there are two further enemies and a cave-in, we will come toward a booth where we can view the open theatre. Snap a picture of the burning seats to contribute to both the Dime Novel Sleuth Achievement & the Real Deal Gumshoe Achievement.

As we continue up the ladder, turn left and head down the hallway. There will be another weapon schematic nestled under a pallet.

Back all the way down the hallway take a right, after the typewriter and break some boxes. Another little secret that was miss-able so I felt it worth mentioning.

The clue is immediately to your left upon entry and the black & white sheet will be a little further in, also on the left side, on a bench.

WEAPON SCHEMATIC #13:

After fighting the 'policemen' and climbing a ladder we will find ourselves in a bedroom. Look immediately to your left and the schematics will be there under what appears to be an oversized bottle of moonshine.

After the police cars pull up on the street and the big enemy hops out, defeat them all and take a left. There will be a big flight of stairs down and at the bottom there is a Baseball card.
This time it is Jedediah Wetters waiting (Batter).

After killing the group of 'policemen', where the train periodically passes by, you go down some stairs. Continue forwards and take a right into a shop and exit out the backdoor. The newspaper will be found on the floor.

You will eat some gruel and beat some officers down with your fists, I see you there Henry Armstrong, and then will make your way up some apartment stairs. Immediately after hitting a jump pad and entering a room, look to your left, the paper will be on a wooden chair.

After shooting the SPIKE-D Target and the pipes fall down, you will find one of the boys in blue finishing a bathroom break. Kill him and then look in the wardrobe. More schematics.

At the point in which we come to a huge hall, transformed into a police office complex, turn left as soon as you can and there will be some boxes. Jump over these boxes and head left. On the desk, hidden behind some papers, will be the comic.
P.I OFFICE

Speak to Stilton and pin all of your clues on the board. Now, go speak to Tammy, in her workshop, before travelling anywhere. Exhaust the dialogue and she will give you the quest.
OLD SUBWAY / CHEESEBALL SUB
MOUSEBERG HERALD #11 & WEAPON SCHEMATIC #20:

After the opening area, where you get ambushed, you will go down some stairs and there will be a couple muscle heads that you need to kill. Turn left, toward the direction where the train was coming from and pick up the ticket booth key. Take this key and go down the platform until you see a locked door. Enter and toggle your flashlight, in the actual ticket booth you will find the paper and in the room immediately to your left upon entering you will find a safe and the schematics on top of some shelves.

MOUSEBERG HERALD #12

In the room with the dug up ground, after fighting multiple enemies, there will be some boxes to the left hand side of the room as you enter. Jump on them and you will be on some unused train tracks. Now head toward the light, into an arched doorway, and the newspaper will be on the boxes.

BASEBALL CARD #11:
Originally posted by Dark Moon Felis:
After the big room with a pit in the ground, immediately after Herald #12, past the next save room & typewriter, head upstairs and turn left at the tracks, pick the door lock, and it's on a clothesline in the secret room.
We have found ourselves Daniel "The Rat-ta-Train" Cruz (Batter).

M-M-MUENSTER KILL!:

It is at the point in which we are in a huge train yard, with lots of exploding barrels if you are yet to get the firestarter achievement, that I got this. I had actually done the achievement numerous times before now as I had timed it but there were enough enemies spawned that it did it without any elemental damage and just went through everyone with a shotgun. I would be surprised if that actually made a difference though.


WEAPON SCHEMATIC #21:

In that same room, down the left, you can jump onto something and then onto a window ledge. Kick the window in and blow up the exploding barrel to destroy the wall. Continue to another opening in the walls, down the sewer passage and head up some stairs and onto a train. Once exiting the train onto the other side, turn immediately left and follow the platform down. At the end there will be a small gap and an overturned carriage. In that little area is another set of weapon schematics. Also if you go into the room and follow it around, there is a pickable safe with plenty of dough in.

MOUSEBERG HERALD #13

After we make our way back through the sewers and kill a couple more goons we will be presented with a typewriter to save at. As we proceed we need to toggle our flashlight and upon entering the second room take a sharp turn to the left and on the crates will be another daily edition of the herald, in the pitch black.

COMIC LOCATION #11:

After your exchange with Vic Gator you will unlock tail-a-chopper. Use this to climb your way up the containers, in front of you, and collect some bread and the 11th comic.

WEAPON SCHEMATIC #22:
Originally posted by Un-evolved Troglodyte & Melon:

This is quite some distance after the above comic, once you reach a typewriter that is between a locked door and a ladder you have found it. Pick the door and pull the switch immediately. Follow the updraft up and navigate to the middle, where there are 4 burning barrels on a platform. Face Southeast and jump onto a suspended train carriage, the blueprints are on a seat.

Now turn around to where it says 'totally normal wall'. Either use the exploding barrel or a stick of D-namite and blow up the wall. The figurine is stood on some wooden shelves to your left.

WEAPON SCHEMATIC #23:
Originally posted by Melon:
After secret collectible #2, drop into the water below, follow the tunnel underwater for another weapon schematic.

Speak to Wanda after getting "Once Upon A Time..." By Tinsel Bros. and she will give the above quest. It is good practice to keep checking dialogue options with every familiar face after story updates and Jack Pepper monologues.
TINSEL AVE. / "ONCE UPON A TIME…" BY TINSEL BROS.
MISSABLE CLUE #6 / TRUCKLOAD OF BLUE:

After navigating through the streets, take a left at the theatre showing 'SWAMP MAN IN COLOR' and at the end of the street will be an overturned cheese truck. Take a photograph and keep hustlin'.

MOUSEBERG HERALD #14:

Soon after you will enter a diner with loads of no-good mobsters. Kill these and check behind the counter, there will be some supplies and also the gazette on the back kitchen top.
