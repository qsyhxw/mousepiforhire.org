Weapons / Boomstick

Mouse PI For Hire Boomstick Guide

Boomstick is the page you open when a fight keeps collapsing into your face. Cross-checked launch guides consistently describe it as the short-range burst weapon with strong pushback, which is why it feels so good against rushing enemies and tight room pressure.

Matchups

Why Boomstick wins the panic-button slot

The simplest reason to carry Boomstick is that it solves a specific kind of problem very efficiently: the enemy that is already too close. When the room compresses and a more methodical weapon no longer has time to breathe, the shotgun's burst damage and knockback give you a fast reset.

That makes it especially useful against heavier melee pressure or swarmy pushes through narrow paths. It is not just about damage. It is about changing the shape of the encounter long enough for you to survive the next second.

It also explains why Boomstick often feels better than its raw range profile suggests. In a game that rewards constant movement, a weapon that creates space can be more valuable than one that simply looks efficient on paper.

Best Use

Where to favor Boomstick

  • Use it when doorways, corners, and short halls keep compressing the fight.
  • Keep it ready if you are struggling with rushdown enemies that punish slower reactions.
  • Pair it with a control or setup weapon such as Portable Freezer if you want safer point-blank follow-up damage.
  • Swap out when the room demands sustained mid-range fire instead of raw stop-power.

FAQ

Quick Boomstick answers

What is Boomstick best at?

Boomstick is best used as a close-range pressure weapon, especially when enemies rush your position and you need immediate space-making force.

Why does Boomstick feel strong in crowded rooms?

Its short-range burst and pushback let it stop aggressive enemies before they fully collapse your space, which is why it often feels safer in cramped fights than precision weapons do.

Which page should I compare it with next?

Compare it with the James Gun if you want the opposite lane of sustained suppression, or the Kiss Kiss page if your concern is wider AOE room clear.
