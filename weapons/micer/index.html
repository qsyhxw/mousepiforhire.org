Weapons / Micer

Mouse PI For Hire Micer Guide

Micer is the weapon that quietly solves more problems than it gets credit for. Launch-week sources consistently frame it as Jack Pepper's reliable starting pistol, and its early burst-style upgrade is the reason it stays relevant after the first few missions.

See Role BreakdownBack to Weapons Hub

Availability

Available from the start

Cross-checked launch guides treat Micer as the dependable sidearm you begin with, so it naturally defines the game's first combat rhythm.

Role

All-round accuracy

It is not the hardest hitter, but it gives stable mid-range control and clean finishing shots without overcommitting ammo.

Upgrade Hook

Burst alt-fire

The most cited early upgrade beat is the three-shot alt-fire, which turns the starter pistol into a better precision follow-up tool.

Best Stage

Early to mid game

Micer is most comfortable before fights become wall-to-wall chaos, though it still has value as a cleanup weapon later on.

Role Breakdown

Why Micer is better than a disposable starter gun

The cleanest way to think about Micer is as the low-friction weapon. You pull it out when you want steady handling, manageable recoil, and accurate cleanup without spending a bigger room-clear tool on a small problem.

That matters most in the first stretch of the campaign, where the player is still learning movement, traversal timing, and enemy spacing. A reliable sidearm buys you breathing room while the rest of the arsenal is still coming online.

The early burst-style alt-fire is what keeps it relevant. Instead of forcing Micer to compete with the game's heavy weapons on raw power, the burst path gives it a cleaner lane: fast, accurate follow-up damage at modest risk. That makes it useful against ordinary enemies who are too dangerous to ignore but not important enough to justify a splash weapon.

Practical Use

When to keep it equipped

  • Use Micer for small patrol fights and scattered targets.
  • Keep it ready when you want to finish damaged enemies without overspending ammo.
  • Lean on the burst alt-fire when a simple pistol shot no longer feels decisive enough.
  • Swap away once fights become heavily packed or demand shield-breaking, freezing, or explosive utility.

FAQ

Quick Micer answers

Is Micer just an early throwaway weapon?

No. Launch-week sources consistently describe Micer as a reliable starter sidearm that stays relevant thanks to its burst-style alt-fire and stable mid-range control.

What is the most important early Micer upgrade?

The most consistently cited early breakpoint is the alt-fire that releases three quick, accurate shots, because it gives Micer cleaner follow-up damage without changing its dependable handling.
