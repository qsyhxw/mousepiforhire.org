Mission Read

What this chapter is really asking from you

Cross-checked newspaper routes place two newspapers in Damp Evidence, one in the opening room by the bed and one on the balcony bench after the boss.

Public Secret Figurine routes agree on a vent path near the dining-room stretch and typewriter, with the figurine on the far table.

Comic-strip and baseball-card routes also overlap here on the post-mine shack and the later hideout-building route reached from the stairs and warp-pipe path.

Checklist

What to prioritize before moving on

  • Check the opening upstairs room immediately instead of assuming this chapter starts empty.
  • After the boss, slow back down for the balcony bench paper and the later hideout cleanup.
  • Treat vents as primary route lines, not decorative extras.
  • If you are tired by the time you reach the hideout, force one more careful room sweep.

FAQ

Quick answers for Damp Evidence

What makes Damp Evidence dangerous on long sessions?

Players often relax after the boss and stop respecting the shack, balcony, vent, and hideout checks that still follow.

Which route landmark is easiest to remember here?

The pairing of the post-mine shack and the later hideout building shows up repeatedly across public collectible routes.

Is this a mission to split across several guides?

Yes. Newspapers, figurines, comics, and cards all have overlapping landmarks here.
