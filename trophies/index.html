Big Picture

What this hub is actually for

A trophy hub should lower your stress, not just duplicate the longest roadmap on the site. So this page focuses on the high-value truths that shape every serious run: platform totals, the hub cutoff, which trophy families create risk, and which supporting pages you should pair together.

The short version is encouraging. MOUSE: P.I. For Hire does not demand a high difficulty route, and the strongest public roadmaps still treat it as a one-run Platinum. The less comforting part is that the route is packed with things you do not want to postpone casually: cards, comics, newspapers, side jobs, clues, and a few combat or misc triggers that are easiest when done in context.

If you want the full step-by-step version, the Platinum Guide is still the main road. This hub is here to help you decide what to worry about before that page gets long.

Fast Facts

The trophy truths worth memorizing

  • No difficulty trophies
  • No New Game Plus
  • No safe post-story cleanup route
  • Jack Squat is the practical hub cutoff
  • Steam hotfixes matter for clue and side-job reliability

Trophy Lanes

The four trophy groups that shape your run

Not every trophy deserves the same kind of attention. The categories below are where the route pressure actually comes from.

Priority Lane

Hub-sensitive progress

This is the category that makes Jack Squat so important. Baseball Cards, Raygun-related progress, side jobs, and anything that wants the hub or office flow should be treated as pre-cutoff work.

Priority Lane

Collectible-linked trophies

Newspapers, Baseball Cards, Comic Strips, and upgrade-related progress all feed trophies or achievements directly. These are the easiest things to underestimate because they look slow, harmless, and very recoverable until they suddenly are not.

Patch Watch

Clue and side-job stability

Steam hotfixes v1.0.4 and v1.0.5 both touched trophy-relevant quest logic. That is a strong reason to distrust older launch-day roadmap wording when it sounds more alarmed than current patch notes justify.

Clean-Up Lane

Misc combat trophies

These are rarely the hardest trophies in isolation, but they become annoying if you leave all of them for the end. The cleanest road is still to do them opportunistically while the right barrel, enemy, or hazard is already in front of you.

Known Trouble Spots

What still deserves caution even after the Steam hotfixes

The cleanest honest summary is this: launch-week trophy guides reported multiple bug-prone trophies, and official Steam hotfixes later addressed some of the most important quest and clue issues. That is good news, but it is not the same thing as a permanent all-platform guarantee that every trophy is now boringly safe.

For a hub page, that means the safest wording stays modest. Side-job reliability improved. Clue tracking improved. But if your route depends on a trophy family that already had a shaky reputation at launch, you still want manual fallback saves and current patch awareness.

This especially matters for Real Deal Gumshoe, because its risk surface is not just one trophy pop. It is the whole chain of side-job availability, completion logic, and hub pacing around it.

Time Estimate

Rough Platinum pace

The most stable estimate is still roughly 20-25 hours, depending on how much collectible cleanup, replay buffer, and side-job verification you need along the way.

That number assumes you are planning. A messy route with trophy re-checks, missed hub windows, or late collectible panic can stretch beyond the tidy guide estimate very quickly.

FAQ

Quick trophy answers

How many trophies are in MOUSE: P.I. For Hire?

PlayStation has 35 trophies including the Platinum. Steam and Xbox use the matching 34-achievement structure without a Platinum.

Can I earn the Platinum on easy difficulty?

Yes. Current roadmaps agree there are no difficulty-specific trophies.

What is the biggest trophy cutoff to remember?

Treat Jack Squat as the practical hub cutoff for side jobs, card-related progress, and other hub-sensitive cleanup.

Are the launch bugs fully solved everywhere?

Steam hotfixes improved clue and side-job stability, but the safest current wording is still cautious. Older bug warnings should be re-read through the lens of those hotfixes rather than repeated blindly.
