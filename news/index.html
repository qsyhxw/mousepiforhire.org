Status Read

What the current public patch trail actually tells us

The launch-week pattern is pretty clear: the game shipped on April 16, 2026, then entered a rapid hotfix phase with multiple public updates in just over a week. The facts are strongest on Steam build timing, hotfix content, and select PlayStation 5 / Xbox version numbers. They are weaker on full platform parity reporting, especially where the public notes stop short of spelling out every Nintendo Switch 2 build.

For players, that means this page works best as a verification layer. If you are troubleshooting a trophy, a blocked quest, a clue that did not register, or a mismatch between Steam and console behavior, this is where the official fix trail starts.

It also matters for route planning. The Platinum Guide, Walkthrough Hub, and Advanced Tips all become more trustworthy when their bug warnings are read through the latest hotfix state instead of frozen at launch-day panic.

That includes late-run cutoff advice. If you are using the Jack Squat Cutoff Guide to protect side jobs, Baseball Cards, or P.I. Office cleanup, this patch page is the right place to verify whether a newer hotfix changed hub lockout behavior, replay assumptions, or other route-sensitive safety rules.

If you are checking whether an older article, sponsored placement, or monetized module still reflects the live game state, this page also pairs naturally with the About, Privacy Policy, and Ad Disclosure pages.

Patch Timeline

The verified public hotfix chain so far

These entries are restricted to fixes we could ground in checked public notes, build records, or current platform-version reporting.

Apr 25, 2026 v1.0.6 Steam build 22923861

Latest public hotfix

The newest official hotfix in the checked record fixes a quest object that could stay stuck in a carry state, improves background-process cleanup when the game closes, and adds a Privacy Policy button to the title screen. The same official note says PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S rollout is planned for the week beginning April 27, 2026.

Apr 20, 2026 v1.0.5 Steam build 22862861

Quest and settings cleanup

Public patch summaries for v1.0.5 center on stability and progression cleanup: black-screen hangs, saved FPS settings between sessions, Tinsel Ave issues, Wallop Bay issues, a blocker in Friends in Deep Places, and an item problem in Slugs in the Shell.

Apr 19, 2026 v1.0.4 Steam build 22852168

Clue and input fixes

This was one of the most trophy-relevant early hotfixes. Official notes explicitly say it fixed clue-collection problems for Our Lesser Brothers and Secrets of the Shrew Shortage, while also cleaning up rebinding issues for WASD, space, and left shift.

Apr 18, 2026 v1.0.3 Steam build 22849803

Boot crash and Curdsville blocker

The checked official note for v1.0.3 targets a launch crash at startup and a progression blocker in Curdsville where an evidence marker could fail to let players proceed.

Apr 16, 2026 v1.0.1 Steam build 22814717

Immediate launch-day cleanup

The first public hotfix landed on release day. It focused on early achievement and interaction issues, including the True Beast Powerup achievement and a broken panel near the autopsy table in Powerpoint.

Platform Differences

How the checked rollout differs by platform

The public record we verified is strongest for Steam, because the build IDs are clean and the official hotfix posts line up well with SteamDB. PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S also appear in the checked notes, but usually as follow-up rollouts rather than the first version anchor. That means the same fix can be public knowledge on Steam before the console timing is fully settled in the same note.

There is one especially useful pattern here: the official v1.0.6 note says the console rollout is planned for the week beginning April 27, 2026, which is exactly the kind of detail players need when a Steam issue looks fixed but their console still behaves like an older build.

The checked hotfix notes do not give us the same neat version-number trail for Nintendo Switch 2. That does not prove parity is missing. It simply means the public notes we checked are more explicit about Steam, PS5, and Xbox than they are about Switch 2 version strings.

Known Issue Status

What looks improved, and what still deserves caution

The cleanest current improvement story is around clue collection and quest-blocking bugs. Official notes for v1.0.4 through v1.0.6 repeatedly target those two areas, and that matters a lot because those are exactly the categories that can poison a collectible route or a trophy run.

The public sources we checked do not maintain a neat unresolved-issues master list. Instead, they read like a chain of targeted fixes. So the safest current stance is this: several high-value launch problems were clearly addressed, but a cautious player should still keep manual fallback saves and stay alert around progression-sensitive objectives until the first major patch lands.

That is also why this page should be read together with the Platinum Guide and Walkthrough Hub. Fix history changes how severe some old warnings still are, but it does not magically turn a no-replay game into a casual cleanup game.
